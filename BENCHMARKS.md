## Honest caveats
- Append and verify are O(n): the ledger rewrites full history and fsyncs on every append. At 500 entries an append is ~46 ms; this is a deliberate durability trade-off, not a hidden cost. For high write rates, batch or shard ledgers.
- Single-writer is enforced by an exclusive lock; concurrent writers fail closed rather than corrupt.
- Numbers are single-machine, single-process; they are a floor for comparison, not a distributed-systems claim.
- The fuzzer reports partial coverage when draws are schema-invalid; throughput counts issued attacks, not only those that reached a decision.
